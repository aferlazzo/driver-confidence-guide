Adventure 2 · Enhanced Edition

Go to the Zoo

Maya drives Alex, Tyler, and Emma to the zoo. The animals are mostly contained. The group’s opinions are not.

Scene 1 of 5
Confidence points: 0/5

Scene 1 · Before departure

Four friends, one route, 14% battery

Emma has the tickets. Alex has snacks. Tyler has brought binoculars powerful enough to inspect the moon.

“My phone has fourteen percent. That is practically fifteen.” — Maya

The zoo is across town, construction has closed the usual exit, and nobody brought a charging cable because everyone assumed somebody else was “nobody.”

What should Maya do before leaving?

A route with a backup

Emma downloads the route and Maya sees the detour before traffic starts moving. Tyler announces that he also knew the way, then points north while facing west.

Instinct takes the scenic route

Ten minutes later they pass the same tire store twice. They pull into a lot, set the route safely, and learn that “near trees” describes most of the planet.

Scene 2 · The fuel gauge

The range estimate begins negotiating

Traffic slows. The low-fuel light appears and the estimated range drops from 28 miles to 19. The zoo is 11 miles away, but the return trip has not magically disappeared.

“We can get gas after the zoo. The car will be lighter after we eat the snacks.” — Tyler

What should Maya do?

Five minutes buys peace of mind

They refuel at a station directly along the detour. Alex buys gum. Emma reminds everyone that gum is not a meal. Two problems are solved; one debate begins.

Open Skill: Pump Gas CorrectlyOpens in a new tab; your Adventure stays here.

The number keeps shrinking

At 12 miles remaining, every red light feels personal. Maya exits for fuel anyway. They lose six minutes and gain the ability to enjoy the zoo without planning a rescue operation.

Scene 3 · Parking

Lot G looks exactly like Lots A through F

The zoo parking lot is enormous. Maya finds a space near a sign showing a cheerful cartoon giraffe. Everyone exits while discussing which animal would make the worst roommate.

“We will remember the giraffe. Giraffes are extremely memorable.” — Alex

What should they do before walking away?

Thirty seconds well spent

Emma photographs “Giraffe G-14.” They lock the car and head inside. Tyler asks whether photographing a giraffe steals part of its soul; nobody takes the bait.

There are nine giraffes

From the entrance they can already see three giraffe signs. Maya walks back, records G-14, and returns before the group becomes an exhibit titled “Humans Making Preventable Mistakes.”

Scene 4 · The bird incident

A direct hit on the windshield

After several hours, everyone returns tired and slightly sunburned. A bird has left an astonishing contribution across the windshield, directly in Maya’s line of sight.

“Technically, we wanted an authentic wildlife experience.” — Emma

The windshield washer removes the edges but turns the center into modern art.

What should Maya do?

Visibility restored

Alex gets water while Maya cleans the glass. Tyler offers binoculars as a substitute windshield and is politely overruled.

Open Skill: Clean the WindshieldOpens in a new tab; your Adventure stays here.

The clean corner is not a windshield

Maya tries the driver’s seat and immediately realizes the smear hides pedestrians near the front-left corner. They clean it before moving.

Scene 5 · The hot car

The temperature gauge climbs in traffic

On the return trip, traffic stalls and the temperature gauge rises above its normal position. A hot smell enters through the vents.

“I assume the car is simply remembering the reptile house.” — Tyler

What should Maya do?

Safe beats stranded

Maya stops before the gauge reaches the red zone. Roadside assistance finds a coolant leak. The friends wait in shade and rank zoo animals by likelihood of owning a timeshare.

Open Skill: Know When to Call for HelpOpens in a new tab; your Adventure stays here.

Traffic refuses to become wind

There is nowhere to drive faster, and the gauge continues upward. Maya pulls over and calls for help before engine damage turns an inconvenient afternoon into a very expensive one.

Adventure complete

Everyone made it home—eventually

  • Plan navigation and power before departure.
  • Refuel before low fuel becomes an emergency.
  • Record where you park.
  • Never drive with obstructed visibility.
  • Stop safely when an engine may be overheating.
