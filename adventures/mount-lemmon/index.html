Escape to Mount Lemmon

Alex and Maya leave Tucson heat for the mountain. The road climbs, the views improve, and the car begins offering a practical examination with no study guide.

What should Alex do?

How should they check?

Oil level is normal. That helps the technician focus on the cooling system.

Scene 4 · The tire gives up

A warning sounds and the tire loses pressure. The shoulder is narrow and traffic rounds a blind curve.

What should Maya do?

Location is part of the repair

They reach a wide turnout slowly and let roadside assistance handle the tire.

The descent requires restraint

What should they do?

The mountain will still be there

They descend within the limit and replace the damaged tire.
