Monsoon on the Way Home

The sky turns purple over Tucson, the temperature drops, and Tyler announces that rain is “just free car washing.” The monsoon has prepared a rebuttal.

Visibility starts disappearing

A dust wall approaches. The windshield is dirty and the washer reservoir is nearly empty.

What should Alex do?

Preparation beats the first raindrop

The windshield is clean and the washer system ready before visibility collapses.

Scene 2 · Water on the road

The tires begin to feel light

Heavy rain covers lane markings. Traffic still moves near the speed limit.

What should Maya recommend?

Space becomes the safety system

Alex slows and gains room to react when traffic suddenly brakes. Adequate tire tread matters too because worn tread reduces wet-road traction.

Scene 3 · The flooded wash

A familiar road becomes a brown river

Two cars turn around. The water depth and road surface are impossible to judge.

What should they do?

Turn Around, Don’t Drown

They reverse safely and choose higher ground. The detour costs time, not a vehicle.

The water has deleted the tracks

Scene 4 · Visibility reaches zero

Rain and dust erase the road

Even at low speed Alex can no longer see lane edges. A lit shopping center entrance is immediately available.

What should Alex do?

The storm gets the road for a while

They park well away from the roadway, turn off the lights so other drivers cannot mistake them for moving traffic, remain buckled, and wait for the worst cell to pass.

The lane remains a lane

Vehicles approach through the rain. Alex moves into the shopping center and waits safely.

Scene 5 · After the storm

The brakes sound different

When the rain eases, the brakes briefly squeal. The pedal feels normal, but debris and water covered the road.

What should Alex do?

Gentle checks provide useful evidence

The sound fades, braking remains normal, and Alex records the concern for the next check.
