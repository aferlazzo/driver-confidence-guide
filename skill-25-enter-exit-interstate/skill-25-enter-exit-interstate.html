Interstate Driving · I-10

Enter and Exit I-10 Safely

Use the full ramp, match speed, select a gap, and plan the exit early. Freeway confidence comes from having fewer last-second decisions.

Before entering

Know your direction and exit. Mount the phone, start directions, scan freeway traffic, and leave your hands available for driving.

Joining the freeway

Build speed

Use the acceleration lane to approach the speed of freeway traffic without exceeding the posted limit.

Select a gap

Signal and adjust your speed to enter behind or ahead of a specific vehicle. Do not assume freeway traffic must move.

Keep checking

Use mirrors and a shoulder check. Watch the vehicle ahead as well as the gap beside you.

Merge smoothly

Enter without stopping, forcing another driver to brake, or crossing the solid painted gore area.

Leaving I-10

Read signs early. Move right one lane at a time, checking and signaling for each lane. Enter the exit lane before reducing to ramp speed. If the exit cannot be reached safely, take the next one—GPS recovers much faster than bodywork.
