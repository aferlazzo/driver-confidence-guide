Interstate Judgment · Passing and Speed

Use the Passing Lane and Manage Speed

The far-left lane is a tool for overtaking—not a home, a racetrack, or a place to teach strangers lessons.

Keep right when you are not passing

Choose a right or center lane that suits your speed and destination. Use the far-left lane to overtake slower traffic, then return right after your vehicle is safely ahead.

Arizona law requires traffic moving below the normal speed under current conditions to use the right-hand lane except while passing. That does not give faster drivers permission to exceed the posted limit.

A safe pass

Check the full gap

Look for rapidly approaching traffic and blind-spot vehicles before moving left.

Stay lawful

Passing does not suspend the speed limit. Wait if the pass cannot be completed safely and lawfully.

Create clearance

Do not cut back in. Wait until the passed vehicle is clearly visible behind you and a safe following gap exists.

Return right

Release the passing lane when the maneuver is complete and it is safe to move over.

Pressure is not a speed limit

If someone tailgates, remain predictable. Do not race, brake-check, or deliberately block. Complete a pass safely and move right when clear. Let the aggressive driver become someone else's weather system.

Drive at or below the posted limit—and slower when traffic, visibility, pavement, or weather makes the limit unsafe.
