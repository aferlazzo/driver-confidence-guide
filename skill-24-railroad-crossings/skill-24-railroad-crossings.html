Railroad Safety · Action Thinking

Cross Railroad Tracks Safely

The critical decision happens before your tires touch a rail: is there enough open space for the entire vehicle to clear the crossing?

Never enter without an exit

Look beyond the tracks. If traffic is stopped and your whole vehicle cannot clear every rail, remain before the crossing—even when the road signal is green and the car ahead moves.

Do not ask, “Can I reach the tracks?” Ask, “Can my rear bumper clear the tracks with room to spare?”

Read the complete crossing

👀

Look and listen

Expect a train or other rail equipment on any track at any time. Freight trains do not follow a schedule drivers can depend upon.

🚦

Obey every warning

When lights flash, bells ring, or gates lower, stop no closer than 15 feet from the nearest rail. Never drive around or under a gate.

🚆

Check every track

One train can hide another. Wait until all warnings stop, gates are fully raised, and all tracks are clear.

➡️

Cross continuously

Once you confirm a clear path, cross promptly without stopping, passing, changing lanes, or becoming distracted.

If the vehicle stalls

  1. Get everyone out immediately—even if no train is visible.
  2. Move far away from the tracks.
  3. Find the blue-and-white Emergency Notification System sign from a safe location.
  4. Call its phone number and provide the crossing ID. If there is no sign, call 911.

Do not remain in the vehicle or gather people on the tracks to push it.
