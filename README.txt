DRIVER CONFIDENCE GUIDE
Version 1.1.0

Live site:
https://aferlazzo.github.io/driver-confidence-guide/

PURPOSE

Driver Confidence Guide helps people who are newly responsible for a car learn practical skills without embarrassment or unnecessary jargon. It is not a repair manual. It helps learners recognize a situation, take a safe first step, use the vehicle owner's manual, and know when to call for help.

AUDIENCE

- Teen and newly licensed drivers
- College students and young adults with a car
- First-time vehicle owners
- Adults who were never taught basic car ownership skills
- Parents and educators helping new drivers

SITE STRUCTURE

- index.html: canonical learner-focused home page
- adventures.html and adventures/: three interactive driving stories
- skills.html and skill-* folders: twenty illustrated practical lessons
- characters/: recurring cast profiles
- feedback.html: reader feedback form
- about-tony.html: creator background
- the-bigger-idea.html: optional background on the Adventure Learning method
- docs/: project, architecture, and deployment documentation

ACCESSIBILITY AND SAFETY

Every published skill includes a native-text quick summary for readers who prefer text or use assistive technology. Vehicle procedures vary; the owner's manual remains the final authority. Safety-sensitive topics tell readers when professional or roadside help is the appropriate next step.
