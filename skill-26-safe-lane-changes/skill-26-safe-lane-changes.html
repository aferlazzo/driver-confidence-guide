Lane Changes · Space Management

Change Lanes Safely

A lane change is a sequence, not a twitch: reason, mirror, signal, blind-spot check, gap, gradual move.

Change lanes for a reason

Pass, prepare for an exit, avoid a hazard, or create space for merging traffic. Constant lane-hopping adds blind-spot exposure while usually saving very little time.

The complete check

1. Look ahead

Confirm traffic in your lane is stable and that you will not run into a slowing vehicle while checking beside you.

2. Mirrors

Check rearview and side mirror for approaching traffic and the size of the gap.

3. Signal

Communicate early enough for others to understand. A signal requests space; it does not claim it.

4. Shoulder check

Briefly check the blind spot beside the rear quarter of the car, then look forward again.

Move only when the whole gap works

Keep a stable speed when practical and steer gradually into the center of the new lane. Cancel the signal. Never cross several lanes in one sweep; complete the first change, reassess, then begin another.
