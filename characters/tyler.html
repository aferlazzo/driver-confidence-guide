Meet the Crew

Tyler

Confidence first. Instructions later.

Funny, optimistic, and hands-on. Tyler likes to understand how things work and is usually the first person willing to try.

“How different could it be?”

First appearance: Episode 1 — Borrow Mom's Car

What Tyler brings

  • Keeps stressful situations from becoming too heavy.
  • Moves the group from talking to doing.
  • Explains practical ideas in straightforward language.

What Tyler is learning

Being willing to act is valuable, but the smartest first move is often to check the instructions and understand the risk.

How Tyler fits the crew

Alex asks the questions Tyler skips, Maya adds a plan, and Emma makes sure enthusiasm does not overlook safety.
